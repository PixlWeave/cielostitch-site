System requirements

Minimum:

  • OS: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • CPU: Dual core Intel or AMD processor
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Display: 1024x768

Recommended:

  • OS: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • CPU: Modern 4-core Intel or AMD processor
  • Memory: 16 GB+ recommended
  • Display: 1920x1080 or higher for comfortable panel review
  • Storage: Large mosaics benefit from fast SSD storage.
