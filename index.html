What we offer

Everything you need to craft the perfect sequence.

🌅

Smart Frame Blending

Our algorithm detects exposure changes and blends frames seamlessly for a natural, flicker-free result.

🎞️

Batch Processing

Drop hundreds of images at once. CieloStitch handles them in parallel, right inside your browser.

One-click Export

Export to MP4, WebM, or GIF at any resolution — no plugins, no server uploads required.

Apply cinematic LUTs or build your own grade with a non-destructive, live preview pipeline.

Motion Stabilisation

Reduce unwanted camera shake with our optical-flow stabiliser, frame to frame.
