## Why Create Solar Mosaics?

Even modern astronomical cameras often have a field of view too small to capture the entire Sun at the desired resolution. By taking a series of overlapping panels and stitching them together, you can create:

* Full-disk solar images at exceptional detail,
* Large active region mosaics,
* High-resolution H-alpha panoramas,
* Ultra-wide views using long focal length telescopes.

Solar mosaics allow you to overcome the limitations of sensor size without sacrificing image quality.

## Typical Solar Mosaic Workflow

### 1. Capture the Panels

When acquiring the data:

* Maintain approximately **20–30% overlap** between adjacent panels,
* Use consistent exposure settings,
* Capture all panels as quickly as possible to minimize changes caused by atmospheric seeing and solar evolution.

### 2. Pre-process the Images

Many solar imagers first process their videos using stacking software (e.g. AutoStakkert, etc.) before creating the mosaic.

Typical pre-processing steps include:

* Frame selection,
* Image stacking,
* Sharpening,
* Contrast enhancement.

### 3. Stitch the Mosaic in CieloStitch

Load the processed panels into CieloStitch and allow the software to determine their relative positions.

Review the alignment and make adjustments if necessary.

### 4. Final Processing

After exporting the mosaic, perform any desired finishing touches in a software of your choice (Gimp, Photoshop, etc.):

* Contrast adjustments,
* Colorization,
* Noise reduction,
* Annotation.
